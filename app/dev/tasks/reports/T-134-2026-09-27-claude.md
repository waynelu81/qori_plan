# T-134 — report

## Outcome

A creator can call a live session off, take that back, and add the next one.
Cancel session on a live row writes `content.cancelled_at` under the row lock
and nothing else, so Undo puts back exactly what was there, a hidden recording
included. It is refused while a Peer can see a recording. From that minute
both pages read cancelled. The Peer's card shows the session's time and "This
session was cancelled." and nothing else; a saved Join link lands on the card
instead of the meeting; and the creator is never nudged about it. Add the next
session copies the title, the provider, the join link, the length and the
Recorded switch to the same local time a week on — or to the first weekly slot
still ahead — through the same `add()` the New Episode form uses, so the
Episode cap, the over-cap lock and the timezone guard apply. Its toast names
the new start in the Group's zone. No email goes out for any of it until
`T-138`.

## Departures

- **Add the next session is offered only on a row with a start**, so a legacy
  row cannot reach `copy()`'s programmer-error refusal (Re-scope log).
- **The panel reads cancelled from `cancelledAt`**, not `state`: a row with no
  start has no state and can still be cancelled.
- **In `cancelled` the panel also hides the Edit toggle** (the spec hid the
  form) **and `T-129`'s recording-email lines** (with the recording list).
- **`docs/tinker/README.md`'s row** and **`withLockedContent()`'s docblock**
  (Added during execution).
