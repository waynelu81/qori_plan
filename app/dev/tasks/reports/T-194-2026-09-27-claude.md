# T-194 — report

## Outcome

The progress panel on a creator's Series page now names the Peers who went
quiet — granted, not finished, and not seen for `stalled_days` — with when each
was last here and whether they can be reminded: "Will be reminded", "Reminded
on …", or why not (no consent, unsubscribed, or an address that cannot receive
email). The owner's one click, "Remind 1 Peer", mails each remindable Peer
one email that opens the first Episode they have not completed. It is a
Stalled campaign on the campaign ledger, so it counts against the day's cap
and the month's allowance, all or nothing, and a Peer is reminded once a quiet
spell. An admin reads the list and "Only the owner sends reminders." Nothing
is ever sent without the click. `qori:mail:check` sends it as its fifteenth
message.
