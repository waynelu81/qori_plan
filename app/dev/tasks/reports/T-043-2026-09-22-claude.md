# T-043 — report

## Outcome

A creator can invite people who have no Qori account. The Series page has an
**Invite people** panel beside the share link, leading to a page of up to ten
rows — email, first and last name, and, for a priced Series, a price that
starts at the Series' own. One paste of a spreadsheet column fills the rows,
names from the next columns included; a percentage off can be written into
every row at once, and any row can be set to 0 for free. The creator picks how
long it lasts, sees when that runs past the last live session, vouches for the
list and sends. Each person gets one email with their own link, bound to their
address; an address already invited says when as it is typed, and one that
already has access is refused. The list below shows every invitation's status
with Send again and Withdraw. The link lands on the Series page remembering
the invitation — accepting it there is `T-181`. The free plan sends ten a day.
