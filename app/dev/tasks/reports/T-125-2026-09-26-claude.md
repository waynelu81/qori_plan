# T-125 — report

## Outcome

A live Episode on a Peer's Series page is a card now, not a dead row. It
shows the start and the end in the device's zone, with the Group's zone after
" · " when the two differ, says "Recorded" or "Live only", and says what comes
next in the schedule's words. From 15 minutes before the start until 15
minutes after the scheduled end it offers **Join on Zoom** (or Teams, or "Join
the session"), a plain `<a target="_blank">` to the Open route, which
redirects to the pasted link; for two hours after that it offers "Still in the
session? Join again", for a "Live only" session too. A Join followed outside
that span comes back to the card with one line. `GET
/shared/{seriesId}/episodes/{episodeId}` is the address every later email and
calendar file will be minted against: the Series page's gate, then that
Episode's anchor. Every redirect that carries a URL writes one `access_opens`
row. A Peer who never saved a timezone is offered their device's zone above
the list, and saving comes back to the same page.
