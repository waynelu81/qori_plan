# T-180 — report

## Outcome

A Group already paying changes its plan on the subscription it has, in the
currency it pays in, and can no longer open a second one:

- **Upgrade** (Start to Pro): the page shows Stripe's own figure for a period
  of Pro less the unused part of Start, charged at once, with the renewal date
  moving to that day. Once paid, the plan changes immediately. A declined card
  or a bank's extra step changes nothing and opens Stripe's page for that
  invoice, where paying applies it.
- **Downgrade** (Pro to Start): scheduled for the end of the period paid for,
  keeping the currency and any voucher. The billing page says when, and "Keep
  Pro" cancels it until then.
- The "Switch to" buttons of a subscribed Group lead to the change page, the
  currency menu gives way to "You pay in EUR, and a change of plan stays in
  EUR", and a second Checkout is refused even if asked for directly.
- The webhook keeps the subscription's id and currency on the Group, and an old
  subscription ending no longer drops a Group to free while its own runs.

## Where the specification was wrong

| Section | Said | Actually |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Code | `changePlan(Group, string $plan): ?string` | It also takes `$destinationUrl`: when Stripe's answer names no invoice page, the owner goes to the portal, which needs somewhere to send them back, and a Service does not build routes. |
| Code | `PlanUpgrade { GroupSubscription $subscription; ?string $paymentUrl; isPaid(): bool }` | `paid` is its own field. "No URL" would otherwise read as "paid" for a declined charge whose answer carried no invoice page. |
| Files | "the spike's five bodies" | Seven: the subscription before the upgrade (`subscription-start-eur.json`) and the schedule as `from_subscription` creates it (`subscription-schedule-created-eur.json`) are what the tests' first calls answer with. |
| Decisions | Silent on how a refused change is shown | Refusals go back to the billing page as a toast (`AppException::redirectTo()`). `change_needs_payment` is a 402, which has no error page, and the billing page is where Manage billing is. Logged under Re-scope. |

## Found, not fixed

- On the billing page, the server renders an amount as "EUR 34.00" and the
  browser as "€34.00". It was seen in the console during the walk, and the
  sidebar mismatches appear on every page → T-182
- A cancelling subscription, one with `cancel_at_period_end`, that is then
  upgraded keeps cancelling, at the end of the new period. Qori's pages do not
  say that a plan is cancelling at all → decided: cancelling stays the
  portal's, where a cancelled plan is renewed. Showing the cancellation on the
  billing page is not this task's.
- A Group that subscribed before this migration has no stored subscription id
  until its next `customer.subscription.*` event. Until then it would be
  offered a second checkout → decided: there are none in production before
  release, and a sandbox one picks the id up on its next event
- The walk left `sages-group`, a design-review Group, on Pro with a sandbox
  subscription (`sub_1UIRGBKUCxslwo7aaMDFTiFt`). The spike left two test
