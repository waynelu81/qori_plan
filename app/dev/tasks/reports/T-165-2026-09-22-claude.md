# T-165 — report

## Outcome

Integrations no longer turns into the 502 page when a Group's Stripe account is
gone. An account Stripe no longer answers for — deleted, closed, or Qori
removed from it — is shown as "Can't be reached": "Qori can no longer reach
this Stripe account. It may have been closed, or Qori removed from it in
Stripe. Disconnect it here, then connect the account you use now." Disconnect
is on the page and works for it (`T-163`). The dashboard's warning and the
price field treat it as payments held back, like any other readiness that asks
the creator. Every readiness that is not ready now shows in the brand's
colours: red with an octagon for refused or unreachable, amber with a triangle
while the creator has steps to take, neutral while Stripe checks.
