# T-064 — report

## Outcome

A Group can let go of its connected account, from either side. On the
Payments page a connected account now offers "Disconnect Stripe" and nothing
else; the "Continue on Stripe" button that used to sit there once the account
was active is gone from that state and stays in the pending one, where the
form may still be unfinished. Disconnect is behind a confirmation that says,
in the Group's own vocabulary, what stops working and how many priced Series
that touches. Confirming clears the id, returns to the page in its
"connect" state with a toast, and the next connect creates a fresh account. A
creator who removes Qori from inside their Stripe dashboard is honoured too:
the webhook now handles `account.application.deauthorized` and clears every
Group holding that id. Owner-only, with the guard and the copy that `T-062`
will reuse for connecting.

## Departures

**The task was re-sequenced before it was claimed.** It first depended on
`T-062`, for a `PayoutsService` and an owner guard that task was to create.
Nothing in a disconnect needs the POST form or the un-pinned country, and
`T-062` carries a sandbox check that could stall it, so this task now creates
the service and the guard and `T-062` builds on them. `T-062` and `T-063` were
rewritten to match, and the stream's order says why. Done as planning, on the
unclaimed specs, not as a re-scope.

**`return()` reads through the service too**, so the controller has one way to
read an account rather than two. `connect()` keeps its method-injected
`SellsSeries` exactly as the task says, until `T-062` moves it.
