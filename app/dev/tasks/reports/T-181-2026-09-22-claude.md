# T-181 — report

## Outcome

Someone invited can take it up on the Series page. Following the email's
link, a person with no account sees who invited them, their own price in
place of the Series' — the Series' struck through beside it — the address it
was sent to shown rather than asked, and their name filled in; the terms and a
code sent to that address let them in. Someone signed in with that address
gets an Accept button instead. Free is granted there and then; a price goes to
Stripe's checkout at the invitation's price, and the payment's webhook marks
the invitation accepted. Anyone signed in with another address is told the
invitation is someone else's, the address masked, and sees the page as
everyone does; an invitation that has ended says so and changes nothing else.
The creator's list shows it accepted.

## Departures

- The code step finds an account whatever the case of its address
  (`SeriesAccessController::start()`): an invitation's address is lowercased,
  and before this a guest typing another case made a second account.
- Accepting a free invitation forgets it; a priced one stays remembered through
  checkout, so someone who comes back without paying still sees their price.

## Found, not fixed

- The design-review Group's connected account, `acct_1UF2SKKUCxNNHqBQ`, is no
  longer one Qori's key can reach — Stripe answers that the key "does not have
  access to account", so nothing priced in the design-review world can reach
  Checkout. It comes from the owner's `QORI_DESIGN_REVIEW_CONNECT_ACCOUNT`. →
  decided: the owner's to re-point, asked in the conversation
- An invitee who already has access sees Open and the invitation stays Waiting
  in the creator's list; marking it on a page load would be a write behind a
  GET. The form already refuses to invite someone with access, so this is
  only someone given access after being invited. → decided: left until a
  creator asks why one says Waiting
- The page's hydration warning on this page is money: the server writes
  "$89.00" and the browser "A$89.00", because `formatMoney()` takes the
  runtime's locale. → T-182
