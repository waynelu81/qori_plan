# T-146 — report

## Outcome

In code, the creator's Stripe account is now called payments, not payouts.
Qori never pays the creator: a Peer pays them on their own account, and
Stripe pays their bank. No step of the Stripe sign-in is called `return` or
`oauth` any more. The Connect Stripe button goes to
`g/{group}/payments/stripe/begin` (`PaymentsController::begin()`). Stripe
sends the owner back to `u/payments/stripe/finalise`
(`PaymentsFinaliseController`), and setup's forwarding address is
`PaymentsDestination`. The billing portal takes a `$destinationUrl`. No text
a person reads has changed.
