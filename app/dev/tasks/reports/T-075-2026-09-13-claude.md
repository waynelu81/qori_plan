## Outcome

Creator setup is three parts, each saying why it is asked and each with "Set
this up later". Part one names the Group and says who sees the name; part
two offers the same two Stripe doors the Integrations page offers, with the
reason above them, and Stripe returns to part three rather than to
Integrations; part three says how files work — documents on Qori, video and
audio linked where they are, Dropbox and Google Drive to come — and moves on.
The Group records what was done or skipped, a sign-in resumes at the first
part with no answer, an admin is never sent through somebody else's setup,
and every Group that already had a name was stamped complete by the
migration so nobody already sharing meets the frame. The dashboard's own
name card, the Integrations page and the disconnect flow are unchanged; the
doors they share are one component now.

## Departures

**`PayoutDoors`, twice.** The two Stripe doors were extracted into a Vue
component and their props into a Support class, used by the Integrations page
and by part two, so the pages cannot drift. The Integrations page's own
markup for the doors is gone and its props come from the same call.

**`SetupSteps` and `PayoutsReturn`** are two small Support classes rather
than constants on the controller: the landing, the rename and the return
handlers all need the same answers.

**Part one can be revisited** while setup is unfinished, so Back from part
two works; only a finished setup sends an owner home from it. `T-068`'s test
that expected a named Group to be sent home from part one now expects it to
render until the other two parts have answers.

**The factory's default Group is set up**, as it is named, with an
`unnamed()` state clearing both. Every test that signs an owner of a factory
Group in would otherwise have landed on part two.
