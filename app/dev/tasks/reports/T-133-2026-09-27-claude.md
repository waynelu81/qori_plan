# T-133 — report

## Outcome

A Peer can put a live session in their own calendar. While a session is ahead,
its card on the Series page offers "Add to calendar", which downloads a
standard calendar file, behind the same access check as the page. The file
holds one event: its start and end as UTC instants, so the calendar shows them
in its own zone; its title naming the session and the Series; and a link to
the session's address on Qori, never to the meeting. Its identity is the
Episode's id and its sequence is the schedule version, so downloading it again
after the creator moves the session updates the entry the calendar already
holds instead of adding a second. A cancelled session reads as cancelled under
the same identity. Downloading records nothing about the Peer, and the file
carries no reminder of its own: each calendar applies its default, and whether
it should is asked of the owner.
