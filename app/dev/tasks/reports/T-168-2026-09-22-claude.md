# T-168 — report

## Outcome

Qori's subscription checkout now offers a business its VAT, GST or ABN field.
Stripe's page shows "I'm purchasing as a business", and the legal name and
billing address typed there are kept on the Stripe customer for its invoices.
The number is never required, and Qori stores none of it. Tax calculation
waits behind `STRIPE_AUTOMATIC_TAX`, off, because Stripe refuses a session
asking for it until the account has a head office address.
