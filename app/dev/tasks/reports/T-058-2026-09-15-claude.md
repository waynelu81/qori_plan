# T-058 — report

## Outcome

Five of the six places that showed an amount now call one function,
`formatMoney()` in `resources/js/lib/money.ts`: the public Series page, the
Series list, Billing, and the admin Creator and Pricing pages. None of them
prices a missing currency in dollars any more. Each narrows on the price and
currency before it formats, and every reachable state renders exactly the text
it did. The public pricing page keeps its own formatter by the owner's
decision. It waits for a design review, and a test names it with that reason
and fails once the page moves. `T-088` is the draft that moves it.

**What the owner changes to get 美元:** in `resources/js/lib/money.ts`, set
`CURRENCY_DISPLAY` to `'name'` instead of `'symbol'`. A zh-CN reader then sees
`39.00美元` where they see `US$39.00` today, and an en-AU reader sees
`39.00 US dollars` where they see `USD 39.00`. The public pricing page would
not follow until `T-088`.
