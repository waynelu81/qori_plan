## Outcome

rows are their `collaborations()`, the one for a given Group is
`collaboratorFor()`, a Group's rows are its `collaborators()`, the service adds
and removes a collaborator, the current one is `CurrentGroup::collaborator()`,
the admin console sends `collaborators` and `collaborations` to the browser, and
the duplicate error is `errors.group.already_a_collaborator`. Nothing about
seats changed: `seatsUsed()`, `seatLimit`, `team_members` and
`billing.limits.seats` are about a plan's allowance and keep their word, as
`T-046` recorded.

The English sentence behind the renamed error key is unchanged, and the key was
removed rather than aliased so a stale caller fails a test instead of rendering
a key to a person.
