# T-117 — report

## Outcome

Every Episode write now decides from what the database holds: `publish()`
counts in the database, `update()` and `remove()` find the Episode with a
query, `remove()` decides its unpublish from the database's count, and
`reorder()` checks and writes the ids the database has. A Series whose Episodes
were loaded before an add no longer refuses to publish, loses sight of the new
Episode, or unpublishes with one still there. And two writes to one Series'
positions can no longer interleave: `add()`, `remove()` and `reorder()`, and a
material's `add()` and `remove()`, take the Series row's lock inside their
transaction and count, check the cap and write positions under it.
