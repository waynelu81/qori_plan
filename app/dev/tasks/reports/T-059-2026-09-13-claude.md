# T-059 — report

## Outcome

The sign-up choice is no longer stored. The register form still asks what
brings somebody here, the answer still decides whether a Group is made, and
that decision is still taken by the listener on the `Registered` event — which
now reads the choice from the registration request it is answering, rather
than from a column on the user. `users.signup_intent` is dropped, and with it
the cast, the fillable entry, the property, and every test and comment that
named it. The enum stays to validate the form and to name the branch, and stops
being treated as a model enum.

Behaviour is identical. Registering with "learn" makes no Group; with "share"
or with no answer, one. What is durable is whether the person has a Group, and
sign-in already lands on that.
