# T-136 — report

## Outcome

The "You're in" email now tells a Peer when the class meets next and where it
talks. When the Series has a live session still ahead, the email names the
soonest one — not the first by position, and never one already under way or
cancelled — with its start in the Peer's own zone and the Group's beside it,
and its title links to that session's address. When the Series has a class
chat the page still shows, a line links the Series page's chat card, never the
invite. Neither line appears when there is nothing to say, so a Series with no
session ahead and no chat sends the email it sent before. `grant()` passes
nothing new, the button still opens the Series page, and no join link or
vendor name reaches the email.
