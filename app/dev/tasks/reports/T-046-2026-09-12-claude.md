# T-046 — report

## Outcome

A creator's Billing page no longer reports how many of their team-member seats
are in use. It said "Team members: 1 of 20" on every paid plan, and nothing in
the product can raise that 1 — there is no route, no page and no controller for
inviting a second person into a Group, so nineteen of those twenty seats could
not be filled by anybody who paid for them. The number is gone from the usage
list and from the digest that supplied it. The cap itself is untouched: it still
sits in config, `Group::seatsUsed()` still enforces it, and the admin console
still counts seats through its own queries, where staff are reading the database
rather than being sold anything.

The deferral behind this was already written in `decisions.md` when the task was
drafted; the entry now says it happened rather than that it will.

## Departures

**`DefinitionList.vue`'s docblock was corrected**, which the task asked for in
so many words: it listed seats among what the Billing page shows. It now lists
peers instead, which the page does show, and says why seats left.

**`Dashboard.vue`'s docblock gained a sentence.** It already explained that
"Your role" and "Team members" left the grid during the redesign. It now also
says the seat figure has left the digest, so the next reader does not go looking
for a prop that was described as merely unrendered.

## Found, not fixed

**Every page logs "Hydration completed but contains mismatches".** Seen on the
Share dashboard, the Billing page and the home page, four or five times per
load. It predates this change — the seat prop was never rendered by anything, so
nothing here could have caused it — and it is not specific to any one page. A
hydration mismatch means the server-rendered markup and the client's first
render disagree, which is how a page ends up showing the wrong state until
something touches it. It needs a task.

**Plan card copy is data nobody owns.** The feature bullets on the Billing page
come from `subscription_prices.features`, free text per row. That is where "20
studio logins" is claimed today, and no code change can stop it — the owner
decides what a plan card says. Worth an owner decision alongside the live Stripe
configuration, which is already on the waiting list.
