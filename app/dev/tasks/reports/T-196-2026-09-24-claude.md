# T-196 — report

Sign in instead." and then "Sign up" is no longer asked "What brings you
here?" with "I want to share" ticked. The page says "You're making an account
to get :label." in its place, and `CreateGroupForNewUser` makes no Group while

the receiving home rather than "What will you share first?". The ordinary
register page, with nothing waiting, asks as before.
