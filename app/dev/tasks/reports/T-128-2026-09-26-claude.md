# T-128 — report

## Outcome

Every Peer with access now hears once when a live Episode's recording is
published. Pasting a recording queues one row per Peer who had access at the
time in `session_notices`, the outbox `D-028` describes, and
`qori:sessions:notify` sends them — by hand for now: the owner will test
Laravel Cloud's delayed queue before choosing what triggers it, so nothing
schedules it yet. What it sends: "The recording of :title is ready", with the session's time in the
Peer's own zone and the Group's beside it, and a button to the Episode's
address, never the recording's link or its passcode. The notice waits for the
card: a recording pasted before Join's window closes is sent at the close,
when the card offers Watch. A second part, a hidden recording, a cancelled
session, a revoked Access, a suppressed address and a row older than the
window each leave a `skipped` row with its reason and send nothing; a send
that fails is retried after 15, 60 and 240 minutes and then left `failed`,
reported, for `T-019`. Each run logs a heartbeat. `qori:mail:check` sends and
reads ten messages.

## Departures

- **No trigger.** The owner decided to test Laravel Cloud's delayed queue
  first, so `routes/console.php` has no line for the command, its docblock
  says so, and `NotifySessionsCommandTest`'s case 5 asserts it is not
  scheduled.
- **The four readings above**, each in the Re-scope log.
- **`SessionNoticeService` takes `LiveSessionService`** as a third
  dependency, for the window and `isScheduled()`.
- **`qori:mail:check` reads the ledger two days on**:
  `sendDue(CarbonImmutable::now()->addDays(2))`, because its session is
  tomorrow (`add()` refuses a start already past) and the notice waits for
  Join's close.
- **`D-028`'s "before" is amended in `decisions.md`** under 26 September, as
  the spec's Notes asked: not after, to the second.
- **The sweep reads its clock in UTC** (`sendDue()`, `countDue()`,
  `scopeDue()`): a datetime is written and compared as its own wall clock, so
  a zoned `$now` counted a row a minute early in the flow-doc writer's check
  and would store `sent_at` hours out. Every caller passes UTC today.
- **`shared.episodes.show` leaves the reachability allow-list**: the email's
  button is the link its reason was waiting for.
- **Six cases beyond the 18** in `RecordingReadyTest` — the wait for the
  card, a session moved later, a live row with no start, and the three re-arm
  cases — and test 12's instrument, which the Re-scope log records for
  `T-129` and `T-138`. Every negative assertion in the three files was made
  to fail once in a scratch copy, 59 in all.

## Found, not fixed

- Until something triggers `qori:sessions:notify`, the Peer's card promises
  an email that does not go: `live.state.waiting` ("you'll get an email when
  it is", `T-125`) and `live.state.overdue.resolution` ("You'll get an email
  if one is added", `T-127`). Queued rows wait in the ledger and go on the
  first run → decided: the owner's, 26 September 2026 — they test Laravel
  Cloud's delayed queue first, and nothing reaches a real Peer before `T-032`
  in any case
- The recipient cut-off is the earliest *visible* recording, so hiding a
  wrong link and pasting its replacement moves it: a Peer granted between the
  two is mailed about the replacement, though the class was told of the first
  link. It is the Decisions' rule, and they left the cut-off for a correction
  to the re-send's task; a line is added there → T-140
- `countDue()` counts a row the run will only postpone — a session moved
  later after its recording was queued — until one run has postponed it, so
  the heartbeat's `due` and the dry run's figure read one high for a run, and
  the claim spends a place on it → decided: rare, diagnostic only, and
  corrected by the next run
