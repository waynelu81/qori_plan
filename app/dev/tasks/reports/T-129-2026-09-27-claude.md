# T-129 — report

## Outcome

A creator whose live session ends without a recording is now told, once. On
the first `qori:sessions:notify` run twelve hours or more after a session's
scheduled end, if it is still waiting or overdue, the Group's owner gets one
email: when it was scheduled to end, in their zone with the Group's beside it;
the two things to do on the Series page; and that the email went to them
alone. It goes through the same ledger as the Peers' recording email, with the
same suppression filter, retry and seven-day window. It is checked again before
it goes, so a retry never says a recording is missing after the creator has
added one or said there is none. The creator's Series page now says, on each
live Episode, how the Peers' recording email went: how many were sent, how
many could not be after Qori tried again, how many are still to go, and how
many addresses Qori no longer writes to. Beneath that it says what "sent"
means. Nothing sends either email on its own until `T-206` gives the command
a schedule.
