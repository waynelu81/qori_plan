# T-169 — report

## Outcome

Staff now set a plan's price in one number, USD, and Qori works out every
other currency. Saving Start at US$39 fetches the European Central Bank's
rates for the day, calculates A$55, €34, £30, C$55, NZ$69 and S$50, rounded up
to a whole unit, and keeps the amounts, the rates and their date on the price.
The console shows them under each price. Editing a price's words keeps its
amounts. A new price, a new USD figure, or ticking "Recalculate at today's
rates" fetches rates again. If the rates cannot be fetched, nothing is saved
and staff are told.

## Where the specification was wrong

| Section | Said | Actually |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decisions, rounding | "`bcmath` does the arithmetic on the rate's decimal string" | Nothing declares `ext-bcmath`, so production might not have it. Integers do the job: a rate in whole millionths times cents stays far inside 64 bits. `DecimalRate` holds the arithmetic. |
| Code | "`PricingController::storePrice()` takes `QuotesExchangeRates` by method injection. … calculates, and writes" | That is orchestration, which the conventions put in a Service, and `T-172` adds publishing to it. `PricingService::savePrice()` does it, and the controller delegates. |
| Tests 8, 9, 13, 14 | Each fakes the rates contract for itself | A route caches its controller, and with it the service holding whatever was bound first. A binding swapped mid-test is never seen by the second request. One fake, steered by properties on the test, replaces the per-test mocks. |
| Tests 8, 13 | "the row's `currency_options` is `D-048`'s Start column" | Postgres `jsonb` keeps its own key order (AUD, CAD, EUR…), so the maps are compared as sets (`assertEquals`), not in order. |
| Copy | Only `errors.pricing.rates_unavailable` | The page gained three labels, which reach it as props from a new `lang/en/admin.php`: no new inline English in Vue. |
| Code, `ExchangeRates` | `final class` | No `app/Data` class is final; it is a plain `class` like the rest. |
