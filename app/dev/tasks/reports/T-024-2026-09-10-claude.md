# T-024 — report

## Outcome

A person and a Group can each say what zone their times mean, and the product
now knows the difference between an answer and an absence. The question is asked
in the first-run card on Share home, beside the Group's name, with the browser's
zone already selected and a line saying that is where it came from; it is asked
again on the profile page for the person rather than the Group. Nothing on any
screen reads differently yet — that was the point, and the last test is what
holds it.

The value nobody asked for is the one that matters: `Group::timezone()` falls
back to its **owner's** zone before UTC. A Group is created for somebody at
registration and nobody opens Group settings on their first afternoon, so
without that step a Melbourne creator would have scheduled their first live
session eleven hours out and been told nothing.

## Departures

**The suggestion note had to become a computed.** Read once at setup, "filled in
from your device" survived the save: Inertia swaps the props and Vue reuses the
component instance, so the field went on claiming the value came from the device
after the creator had saved it themselves. It now also stops applying the moment
they pick something else, which is the point at which the field is no longer
pre-filled with anything. There is no JavaScript test runner in this project, so
this was caught by looking and could not have been caught any other way.

**`Timezones::fallback()` is a fourth method the spec did not name.** The spec
said "every consumer falls back to `config('app.timezone')` explicitly", which
is right, but written literally at each call site it becomes four copies of one
fact. One method, and the callers are findable.

**`hasChosenTimezone()` on `Group`, mirroring `hasChosenName()`.** The interface
has to tell "resolved to UTC" from "nobody has answered", and `timezone()`
cannot: a Group whose owner sits in London resolves to UTC honestly. Without
this the form would re-suggest the browser's zone over the top of a deliberate
answer every time the page loaded, which is the silent capture the task's own
decisions forbid.

**`groups.timezone.saved` only wins when the name did not change.** The
first-run card submits both fields at once, and a creator who has just been
asked to name their Group should be told the name landed. Both keys are used;
neither is dead.

## Found, not fixed

- **The Group's zone is asked for on Share home, not in Group settings.** There
  is no Group settings page other than the vocabulary form (`T-004`), so the
  first-run card is the only place it can live today. When `T-026` builds the
  first-run checklist it should take this field with it.
- **`config('app.timezone')` is read in three other places** unrelated to this
  task. They are all correct today because everything is UTC. `T-025` is the
  task that has to look at them.
- **Nothing consumes either value yet.** That is `T-025` for display and `T-029`
  for live sessions, and `T-029` is the one with a user-visible bug behind it.
