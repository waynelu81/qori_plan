# T-052 — report

## Outcome

Signing in now lands on the Group the person last opened, whichever of the four
ways in they used. With no history and one Group, they land on it; with no
Group at all they land on the receiving home, exactly as before, because that
page already lists every Group and offers **Start sharing** and is the
switchboard for anyone with nothing to remember. A passkey sign-in with nothing
intended no longer lands on the marketing page.

The Group is remembered on the user row the moment Group context is
established, written only when it changes, so it survives signing out. Every
path keeps `intended()` on top, so a guest sent to sign in from a protected
page still returns to that page. `/dashboard` is untouched and never
redirects, and registration is untouched.

## Departures

**`SignInLanding::for()` looks the Group up and then checks membership**, rather
than joining through `collaborations()`. Two queries instead of one, on a path
that runs once per sign-in. It reads as the rule it implements.

**`down()` uses `dropConstrainedForeignId()`.** One call that drops the
constraint, the index and the column together, rather than the two the spec's
shape implied.

## Found, not fixed

Out of scope by decision (`T-026` owns the first-run frame), and a new account
has no last Group anyway. It will look, to somebody testing, like this task did
not work — worth knowing before it is reported as a regression.
