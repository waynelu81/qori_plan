# T-113 — report

## Outcome

A creator connects payouts one way: Stripe's sign-in, where they choose the
account they have or open one. Qori no longer opens a Stripe account, mints an
account link or asks a country, and the create door, its refresh and return
routes, `ConnectPayoutsRequest` and the country list are gone. In `Connect`,
onboarding is `beginOnboarding()`, `finaliseOnboarding()` and
`declineOnboarding()`, which `finaliseOnboarding()` answers with when the
creator stops partway; Stripe's landing words are read there and nowhere else.
A landing Stripe refuses tells the creator nothing was connected, on Qori's
own page, instead of reporting an outage, and every landing spends setup's
forwarding address. Without an OAuth client id, the Integrations page and
setup part two say payments cannot be connected yet, and starting the sign-in
is refused. An unfinished account links to the creator's Stripe dashboard.

**For the owner:** nobody can connect payouts anywhere until Connect OAuth is
enabled in the Stripe dashboard (sandbox, then live), the redirect URI
`/u/payouts/stripe/return` is registered for each host
(`http://localhost:8001` locally), and `STRIPE_CLIENT_ID` is set in `.env` and
in Laravel Cloud. `PLAN.md`'s "Waiting on the owner" lists "Connect OAuth in
the sandbox (`T-063`)" beside the live Stripe setup; it now blocks getting paid
at all (`D-023`), and the line is the owner's to change.

## Departures

Listed under **Added during execution** in the task file.

- `resources/views/errors/400.blade.php`, `422.blade.php` and `502.blade.php`,
  so the refusals and an unreachable Stripe show Qori's page. Each uses its
