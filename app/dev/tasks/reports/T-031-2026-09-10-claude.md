# T-031 — report

## Outcome

A Series can be changed after it is created. There was no route that did it:
`share.series.*` registered index, store, show, publish, archive and unarchive,
and `SeriesService` had create and the lifecycle methods and no update. A title
typed wrong at creation was permanent, a summary written before the Series
existed could never be revised, and the only escape was rebuilding the whole
thing under a new slug.

`PATCH /g/{group}/series/{seriesId}` now takes a title, a summary and an hours
figure, and a panel on the Series page submits them. The slug does not move,
which is the rule that makes the rest safe: it is in every link a creator has
already sent and in every access email already delivered.

Price is untouched and still unreachable, deliberately. `SeriesService::create()`
accepts `price_cents` and `currency` and no form has ever sent them, so adding
them here would have smuggled a new capability and an unanswered product
question about people who have already paid in under a bug fix.

## Departures

**The panel is hidden while a Series is archived** rather than shown disabled.
The service refuses an archived Series, so a visible form there would be a
control that answers with a toast. The archive panel at the foot of the page
already offers the way back, which is the action that actually applies.

**The details panel sits above the progress chart**, which the spec did not
place. A creator who has noticed a typo in the title should not scroll past a
chart to fix it.

## Found, not fixed

- **`EpisodeController::update()` still validates only `title` and
  `is_preview`.** An Episode's start time, provider and reference cannot be
  changed, so a live session scheduled for the wrong hour has to be deleted and
  re-added. Raised in `T-029`'s report as well; it needs its own task, because
  it is a second form and a second timezone conversion.
- **A Series still cannot be priced.** `price_cents` and `currency` have no form
  anywhere. Deliberately out of scope here, and it is a real gap with a product
  question inside it.
- **Renaming changes the generated cover.** The initials come from the first and
  last word, so "Watercolour for Beginners II" became "WI". Correct per `T-001`
  and slightly surprising the first time you see it.

## Evidence

`evidence/` holds nothing. Described instead: the Series page carrying a new

name in an input, the summary in a counted textarea, hours below it and a "Save

for Beginners II" with a success toast naming the new title.
