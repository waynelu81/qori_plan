# T-062 — report

## Outcome

Connecting payouts is a POST that creates once, and Stripe's refresh is a GET
that never creates. The owner picks a country on the Integrations page, the
Inertia form posts it, the account is created with that country and nothing
else Stripe can ask for itself, and the browser is sent to Stripe's form
through `Inertia::location()`. Stripe's `refresh_url`, and the pending
state's "Continue on Stripe", are a plain GET that mints a fresh link for an
account that exists and sends anybody else back to the page. `PayoutsService`
is the only writer of `groups.connect_account_id`; the integration's
`onboardingUrl()` takes an id and structurally cannot create. Both writes are
owner-only, and an admin reads why in place of the button.

The task was stopped and rewritten before any of that was built. Its first
version wanted the country left to Stripe's form as well, and the sandbox
refused every shape without one. The re-scope log in the task quotes Stripe;
the rewrite is what was built.
