# T-089 — report

## Outcome

Opening an Episode on a Peer's Series page is now a plain link. Each row that
opens in a tab is an `<a target="_blank">` to
`GET /shared/{seriesId}/episodes/{episodeId}/open` (`shared.episodes.open`).
That route passes the same gate the play route passes, through a new private
`PlaybackTicketService::admit()`. It records the open and then redirects: to
the short-lived signed ticket for a `cloudflare_r2` Episode, and to the
vendor's link for anything else, now wrapped in a `VendorLink` with no expiry.
The click opens the tab itself, so no popup blocker can swallow a
`window.open()` made after an `await`. A `VendorLink::blocked()` answer sends
the Peer to `shared.show#access` with an info toast
(`shared.vendor_notice.redirected`). Nothing produces a blocked link yet;
`T-091` will. The Vimeo embed keeps its fetch-and-`<iframe>` path on the play
route. A live Episode renders its row with no control, and so does the
Continue line.
