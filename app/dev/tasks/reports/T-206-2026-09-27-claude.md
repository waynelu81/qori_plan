# T-206 — report

## Outcome

The scheduler runs `qori:sessions:notify` every thirty minutes, the owner's
choice on 27 September 2026, so the Peers' recording email (`T-128`) and the
creator's nudge (`T-129`) leave on their own. Until now both were only sent by
hand. A notice leaves within thirty minutes of falling due, plus the under a
minute Cloud's scheduler was seen to run late (`T-204`). One run goes at a
time: `withoutOverlapping(15)` and `onOneServer()`. The overlap lock lasts
fifteen minutes instead of Laravel's day, so a run killed outright costs
nothing past itself instead of a day of no email. The command's docblock, the
live-sessions flow and its tinker recipe now say what runs it. Cloud reads the
schedule when it deploys, so the push to `main` is what switches it on.
