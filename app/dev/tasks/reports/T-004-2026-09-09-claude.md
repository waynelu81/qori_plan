# T-004 — report

## Outcome

A Group on Pro can now name its own things, and the words reach everywhere
without any screen knowing it happened. Setting Series to Trail and Peer to
Ruffy changes the navigation, the page titles, the Series list, the stat tiles,
the next-action card and the flash messages — because all of them were already
reading from the resolver. The terminology layer had been a shape with no door
since it landed; this is the door.

The preview leads the page rather than the fields, because the decision being
made is "how will this read", and a column of ten text boxes does not answer
that. It shows real product sentences with the typed words in them, live.

## Departures

**One test beyond the eleven specified**, asserting the nav gate in both
directions.

**A defect in another task's copy, fixed rather than handed on.** With Episode
renamed to Practice, the Share dashboard read _"It has 1 Practices"_ — the
`share.next.make_ready` detail line interpolated `:count` beside
`:episode_plural`, and a placeholder cannot agree with a number. It was
invisible while the noun was regular English and this task made it obvious.
Reworded to drop the count, which the Series page shows anyway three words from
the button the sentence is about. The alternative — making it a `trans_choice`
line — would have `ShareDigest` resolving one of its three strings differently
from the other two, for a number nobody needs there.

I fixed it rather than filing it because it is one lang line with no code
behind it, and the process's "found, not fixed" rule exists to stop tasks
growing, not to ship a sentence that is wrong in front of the person who just
paid for the feature that revealed it.

## Found, not fixed

**Downgrade behaviour is undecided and the current behaviour is a default, not
a decision.** A Group that lapses from Pro falls back to Qori's words and keeps
its stored labels untouched, so returning to Pro finds them where it left them.
That is defensible and it is what `Terminology::resolve()` already did. The
open question in `terminology-refactor.md` — keep read-only, or revert — is
still open.

**`creator` is the one noun with nowhere to show.** Public attribution is
"Shared by {Group}" by design, and the spec is explicit that a custom creator
label should appear as independent metadata rather than in a generated
sentence. Nothing renders it today. The field is in the form because storing an
incomplete set is refused, which means somebody must type a word that currently
does nothing. Its hint says "rarely shown", which is honest and not
satisfying. `T-005` or the redesign should give it a home or the spec should
drop it to four nouns.
