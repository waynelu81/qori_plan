# T-164 — report

## Outcome

When a creator's Stripe account cannot take card payments, Qori now says
exactly what Stripe wants and where. Integrations lists each step under the
page in the creator's own Stripe dashboard that asks for it — finish Stripe's
sign-up, send the document it asked for, fill in details, accept the terms,
add a bank account, answer Stripe's question, turn on cards, or contact Stripe
support — with what Stripe asked for in plain words ("your date of birth", "a
photo of your ID: passport, driving licence or national ID card") and a link
that opens the page. The owner also reads Stripe's own words about anything it
rejected. "Nothing to do" is said only while Stripe is checking. Connecting an
account that cannot charge says where the list is, and the dashboard and the
price field say payments are held back, from what the last read remembered on
the Group. The Peer's side is unchanged, as the owner asked.
