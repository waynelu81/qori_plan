# T-068 — report, second attempt

## Outcome

The first attempt shipped the setup page and a landing that eight tests said
reached it; the owner then registered for real and landed on `/dashboard`. The
cause was the intended URL. Fortify sends every new registration to the home
route, the `verified` wall bounces that to the notice and stores the home route
as `url.intended`, and the verification landing honours an intended URL above
its fallback, as `T-008` decided it should. So the setup page was skipped by a
page nobody chose. Now an intended URL that is the home route is discarded
before the landing is chosen, and only that one: a real page somebody was
bounced from, such as the Series they came to buy, still wins. A ninth case
registers through the real form, bounces off the home page the way the owner
did, and asserts setup.

## Departures

**Only the verification landing discards the home route.** The sign-in
responses keep honouring it. A guest who types `/dashboard` and is sent to
sign in did choose that page, and the sign-in path stores nothing on a normal
sign-out and sign-in, so the setup page is reached there without help.

## Found, not fixed

- Fortify's register response sends an unverified person to the home route
  and lets the wall bounce them. A register response that went to the notice
  directly would store nothing and make this discard unnecessary. Worth doing
  when the auth responses are next touched; not needed for the fix.
  → decided: not worth a task — `SignInLanding::isHome()` discards the home
