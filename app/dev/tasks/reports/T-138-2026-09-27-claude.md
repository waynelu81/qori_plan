# T-138 — report

## Outcome

On a plan that sends them — Start, Pro and School; Free sends none — every
Peer with access is now reminded of a live session the day before it, by email,
with the start in their own zone and the Group's beside it, whether a
recording will follow, the calendar file attached, and a button to the
session's card. The sweep that runs every thirty minutes queues the reminders
and sends them in the same run; a Peer granted later is reminded by the next
run while the session is more than an hour away. Moving a session's time or
length deletes its reminders so they go again for the new time, with no
"moved" email. Cancelling a session tells, once, every Peer who was already
reminded of it, and nobody else; Undo tells nobody anything and reminds nobody
twice. A reminder for a session already started, for a Group gone to Free, for
a revoked Access or a suppressed address is skipped with its reason. The
Peer's card says a reminder is coming until it is due, and the creator's edit
form, cancelled line and cancel toast say who is told, by plan.
