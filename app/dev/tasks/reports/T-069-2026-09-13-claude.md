# T-069 — report

## Outcome

`php artisan qori:reset` drops every table, migrates, and leaves the local
database in one of three states: `clear`, an empty schema; `basic`, the three
plans and the coupon; `full`, the design-review cast with its plans. It asks
which when not told, confirms before dropping unless `--force`, refuses any
environment but local (and testing, for the suite) and any database host that
is not a loopback address, and ends with a row count and a reminder that
storage was not touched. The plans now live in `PricingSeeder`, idempotent, so
"empty but with plans" exists as a state and the billing and pricing pages
have something to list on a fresh database.
