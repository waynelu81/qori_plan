# T-186 — report

## Outcome

The "you're in" email is no longer transactional mail. Once
`QORI_MAIL_BROADCAST` names a real mailer — SES in production — it goes
through it from the campaign domain, under Qori's name, with replies still
going where they went before. Sign-in codes, magic links, verification,
password resets, email changes and invitations stay on Postmark. It still
reaches everyone who gains access, whatever their consent, unsubscribe or
complaint; only a permanent bounce stops it, and the access is granted either
way. Until SES is set up the broadcast mailer is `log`, and the email stays on
the default mailer rather than disappearing into a log, so nothing changes
for anyone — locally or in production — until the owner sets
`QORI_MAIL_BROADCAST=ses`.
