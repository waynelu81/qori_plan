# T-072 — report

## Outcome

A creator can say, on the Series form, what a payment is called and how it
reads on the buyer's card statement. The form explains both at length: the
description is what the creator sees beside the payment in Stripe, so a
Series is recognisable without opening the payment; the statement line is
what the Peer sees days later, and a line they recognise is the difference
between a payment remembered and a payment disputed, with the dispute being
the creator's under direct charges. The suffix field shows the cap, a live
count, and a preview of the whole line as Stripe will compose it. Stripe's
rules for the suffix are enforced before Stripe is asked, each with a refusal
naming the rule, and the cap follows the creator's own prefix once an account
read has carried it. Checkout sends the description, or the title, and the
suffix only when there is one.
