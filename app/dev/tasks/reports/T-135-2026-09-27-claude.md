# T-135 — report

## Outcome

Someone deciding whether to pay for a live course now sees the next session
above the buy controls on the public page: its title, its start and end in
their own zone with the Group's beside it when the two differ, and the
creator's switch as one sentence — "Recorded — watch later if you can't
attend." or "Live only — this session won't be recorded." It shows for every
viewer, and never carries a join link. Every session row on the page shows the
Group's zone beside the reader's. On the creator's Series page a live row in
`upcoming`, `open`, `ready` or `cancelled` offers **Copy message for your
chat**: the session's title, its start in the Group's zone with the zone
named, a line sending each reader to the Series page for their own time, and
the Episode's Qori address, never the meeting link. Where the clipboard is
refused, the row says so and shows the message to select; **Show the message**
opens it anyway. A row in `waiting`, `review`, `overdue` or `not_recorded`
offers nothing.
