# T-001 — report

## Outcome

A Series is recognisable on all five surfaces. The same mark — its initials,
generated from the title — appears on the Share list, the builder, the peer's
home, Shared with me, the peer's Series page and the public page, so a creator
can find the thing they made on the page they send to somebody else.

Three shared parts rather than one card: `SeriesCover` for identity,
`SeriesStatus` for the creator's question, `SeriesProgress` for the Peer's. No
component takes an `isPublic` or `showProgress` flag, which the brief forbids by
name.

| Browser, light                     | Share list, builder, public page at 1100×850 | Same "WB" mark on all three         |

**Could not verify:** 360px. The covers are fixed-size and sit in flex rows that

`T-003` is the QA pass and this is exactly what it is for.

Also unverified: a title that produces no initials — an emoji-only or
punctuation-only Series name renders an empty mark by design rather than
crashing, and that path was reasoned about, not run.

## Where the specification was wrong

| Section | Said                                | Actually                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Code    | Initials from "the first two words" | Wrong for titles. `getInitials` takes first and last, which gives WB for "Watercolour for Beginners" rather than WF — English puts a stop word in second position more often than in last. Reused it, as the spec said to check. |
| Files   | Four progress bars become one       | Three. The fourth is the creator's per-Episode distribution — a different measurement wearing the same shape, with no label. Left alone.                                                                                         |

Neither met the re-scope bar; both change which letters or which files, not
what is built.

## Departures

**A plan-cap bypass, found and closed.** Wiring the builder's header meant
deciding what an _archived_ Series shows there, and the answer was that it
showed "Make ready to share" — and that publishing straight from archived
skipped the cap check `unarchive()` performs. Archive one Series to get under
the cap, publish it again, and the Group is over the cap with nothing having
refused. Reproduced in tinker before fixing:

```
archived, seriesUsed=0
PUBLISHED an archived series — status now published, seriesUsed=1
```

`SeriesService::publish()` now refuses an archived Series, the builder offers
restore rather than publish, and a test covers the order. This is T-009's
defect; its own tests passed because none of them tried archiving and then
publishing. Fixed here rather than filed because it is a plan-limit bypass,
which is the kind of thing that should not wait for a task to be written.

`PageHeader` gained a `#before` slot. A prop would have made the shell know what
a Series is.

## Found, not fixed

**`SeriesStatus` and the public page disagree on purpose, and that is worth
watching.** Creator surfaces show the Series' state, Peer surfaces show the
reader's progress, the public page shows neither. It is the right answer to
three different questions and it will look like drift to whoever reads the
three files next — which is why the reasoning is in the task, in the component
docblock, and in a test asserting the public page sends no `status` at all.

**The cover is deterministic, so two Series with the same initials look alike.**
Acceptable while a Group has a handful; a creator with "Watercolour Basics" and
"Watercolour Beyond" gets two identical marks. The fix when it matters is a real
cover image, which this task deliberately did not build.
