# T-065 — report

## Outcome

`GroupService::createFor()` refuses to create a Group for a person who already
owns one, with a `LogicException` that names the decision. Nothing a person
can do changes: registration still creates one Group, a learner who starts
sharing still gets one and keeps it, and an admin on somebody else's Group who
starts sharing is taken to that Group rather than given one of their own.
Three tests now say so, one from each direction.

## Departures

**Case 1 asserts inside a `finally`**, so the count is checked even though the
call throws. The spec said "one Group exists afterwards" without saying how to
get past the exception; this is the how.
