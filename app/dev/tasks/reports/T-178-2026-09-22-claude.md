# T-178 — report

## Outcome

A Peer no longer has to agree to the creator's emails to get a Series. Every
way in — the stranger's form, the signed-in free button, checkout — asks them
to agree to the terms for the Series instead, shown on the page under "Read the
terms": Qori's own agreement, in the Group's words, until creators can write
theirs (`T-179`). The email box is still there, unticked and marked optional; a
Peer who leaves it gets in and is not a campaign recipient. The Access records
which terms they agreed to and when, and a paid Peer's choices ride with the
payment to the webhook that grants the access. Pressing on without the terms
says why under the box, where the button used to sit greyed out.

## Where the specification was wrong

- **§9 of the product spec**, and `ConsentTest`, said consent is asked
  "explicitly and blocking". `D-049` reverses the blocking half at the owner's
  word; the test is rewritten around the new rule.
- **Checkout never checked a box on the server**: the consent box gated the
  paid button only in the browser, and `CheckoutController` took a plain
  `Request`. `StartCheckoutRequest` checks the terms now.
