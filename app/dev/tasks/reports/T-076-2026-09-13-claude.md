finished, and when the walk ended, sit on the User; the Group keeps only the
facts the walk is read beside, a chosen name and a connected account.

like a fresh creator. And the home routes honour it: an owner with an
unfinished part who reaches the home route or the Group's home, by any door,
is sent to that part. That closes the tab the owner found: Fortify's
verification page, refreshed once verified, redirects to the home route, and
the home route used to render the home.
