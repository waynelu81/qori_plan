# T-077 — report

## Outcome

Sign-in asks who you are before how you prove it. One email field sits at the
top of the page, always mounted; directly under it the choice between
Password and Email link; under that only the chosen method's controls.
Passkey is untouched below the rule, and the exit to registration is still
last. The composition is one form whose action follows the choice, so the
address is typed once, survives the switch, and never leaves the keyboard's
place. Choosing a method clears the other method's errors. The page's
instruction now belongs to the form and leaves with it when "Check your
email" replaces it, which closes the line `T-036` recorded as found, not
fixed.

## Departures

- **`Tabs` takes `:model-value` and `@update:model-value` rather than
  `v-model`.** The switch has to call the form slot's `clearErrors()` at the
  same moment, and a `v-model` cannot carry a second call. The spec's
  `choose(value, clearErrors)` implied this; it is stated here so nobody
  "simplifies" it back and loses the clearing.
- **The report is dated the 14th.** The owner's review was on the 13th and
  the work crossed midnight.

## Found, not fixed

- **The page mixes two verbs.** The title and the submit button say "Log
  in"; the instruction, the link button and the passkey button say "sign
  in". Pre-existing; the new sentence followed its neighbours rather than
  the title. One pass over the auth pages should pick a verb.
- **"Use a different email" keeps the same email.** `LinkSent` returns to
  the form with the address still filled and Email link still chosen.
  `T-036`'s spec also asked for "a way back to Password" there, which the
  component never had. Both are `LinkSent`'s, and out of scope here.
- **The 2FA challenge is still unexercised in a browser**, as `T-036` said.
