# T-170 — report

## Outcome

Qori's pricing and billing pages now show plans in the visitor's currency, and
checkout charges the same figure. Both pages have a currency menu:
- USD, EUR and AUD always, plus the visitor's own currency when it is none of
  those, with the visitor's own selected;
- a fixed currency shows its fixed price, and checkout names that currency, so
  Stripe's page matches;
- a currency without a fixed price, such as JPY, shows US$39 with a line
  saying checkout charges it in their currency, and checkout leaves it to
  Adaptive Pricing.

The country comes from Cloudflare's header and, failing that, from the
browser's language.

## Departures

- `VisitorCurrency::props()` builds the props both pages share. It adds a
  `baseCurrency` prop, so a page can fall back to the USD figure without
  naming USD itself.
- `CurrencyOffer::isFixed()` and `options()`.
- Symfony gives every test request `Accept-Language: en-us`, so the
  "unplaced" test sends a language without a region.
