# T-188 — report

## Outcome

Stripe's two live webhook endpoints now each have a URL and a secret of their
own. Qori's own account posts to `/webhooks/stripe`, checked against
`STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`; creators' connected accounts — a Peer's payment, a
creator removing Qori — post to `/webhooks/stripe/connect`, checked against the
new `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. Each refuses the other's signature. The
signature check itself moved out of the controller into
`app/Integrations/Stripe/Webhooks::verify()`, beside the signature `qori:e2e`
already built there. Before this, a Peer's live payment would have arrived on
the Connect endpoint signed with a secret Qori never checked, and been refused.
