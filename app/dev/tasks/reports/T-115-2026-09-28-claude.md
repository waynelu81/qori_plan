page with the exception's own words: `render()` uses the layout for every
status, where it used to fall through to the framework's bare page for any
status with no view — 401, 402 and 504 today. A status the framework raises
itself and Qori has no page for lands on the new `errors/4xx` or `errors/5xx`,
Qori's page with generic words and the status's own number. And the design
review renders every error page there is, `T-113`'s three and the two
fallbacks among them, where it rendered five.
