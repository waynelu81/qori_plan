# Lane: journey

**Asks.** Can a person get from one end of the north-star loop to the other
without being stranded, misled, or sent somewhere they cannot act?

> Create Series → add Episode → make ready → share or invite → get access →
> continue → finish.

**Needs.** A browser somebody is driving, and the fixtures. This lane is a
walkthrough, not a screenshot set — the screenshots say what each stop looks
like, and only walking it says whether the stops connect.

**Evidence.** Written up in [`../walkthroughs.md`](../walkthroughs.md), which is
where evidence about the _product_ goes. The pass cites it.

## Checks

- **One primary action from a standing start.** A new creator starts a real
  Series from Share without landing on a second empty page first.
- **One clear next step at every activation state**, and the button goes to the
  control that completes it — never merely to the page containing it.
- **No action that ends in a predictable refusal.** In the release gate, and the
  reason the [state](state.md) lane keeps a non-owner identity around.
- **Blocking state outranks encouragement.** A locked Group is told it is
  locked before it is invited to do something it cannot do.
- **Detours return.** Verification, password confirmation and magic-link
  sign-in all come back to the page that sent the person away, carrying the
  destination they were trying to reach.
- **The Peer half.** An invited person understands what they have been given,
  how to get access, and how to continue. Invitation-to-access is one of the
  measures the plan says to instrument.
- **Money keeps its promise.** After payment, fulfilment is not refused by a
  plan limit.

## What it cannot see

- **Anything about a real person's understanding.** The release gate wants five
  representative creators doing this without help, and that is not this lane —
  it is the thing this lane exists to be ready for.
- **Email.** The loop passes through an inbox twice and local mail goes to the
  log. Delivery is the `delivery` stream's problem, but a journey pass that
  skipped the mail steps has skipped part of the journey and should say so.
- **Timing.** Whether a step feels slow is not visible in a walkthrough somebody
  is concentrating on.
