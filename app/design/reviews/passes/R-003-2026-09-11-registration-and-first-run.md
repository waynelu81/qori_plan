# R-003 — registration and the first signed-in visit

Onboarding is partly built and already planned, but the first destination does
not follow the signup choice. A new creator lands in the receiving home; a
person registering from a public Series can lose that Series on verification.
The existing first-Series action works once the creator finds their Group.

## Scope

**Lanes run:** [journey](../lanes/journey.md), limited to direct registration
for Share and Learn, verification from local logged mail, creator navigation
through saving the first draft, and the public-Series registration/verification
return. This is not a completed north-star-loop pass.

**Lanes not run, and why:** surface and state were not systematically reviewed
across their matrices; responsive was not run at the required widths or zoom;
keyboard/focus and contrast were not tested; copy was not audited against the
complete terminology sources. Screen observations below support the journey
findings, not those other lane sign-offs.

**Surfaces:** registration, verification, receiving home with no access, new
Group home, first-Series form and saved draft, and one free public Series. No
paid access, delivery provider, actual invitation email, Episode completion or
certificate journey was run.

## Held up

- Share and Learn lead to different provisioning: the creator received a Group;
  the receiving accounts did not show creator Group membership. This was also
  checked in the registration/listener source and existing test definitions.
- Direct registration reaches a verification notice with resend and logout
  controls. Local verification links verified the intended review accounts and
  returned to signed-in pages.
- Public-Series context survives the registration step itself. F-1 is the
  subsequent immediate verification step, not a claim that every return fails.
- From Group home, **Create Series** opens the minimal form directly at
  `#new-series`. Saving a title produces a real draft, shows the Episode form,
  and reports that an Episode is needed before sharing. No Group-name,
  timezone, payment or storage setup blocked that first draft.
- Existing Group-name/timezone controls and `ShareDigest` next-action logic are
  foundations to reuse. Onboarding is not missing from the plan: T-026/T-027
  already exist as drafts, and T-024/T-029 are completed work.

## Could not see

Real inbox delivery or understanding by representative users; the branch that
presses **Get access** before verification; a different browser/device; existing
access attached during signup; a complete Episode-to-share-to-finish loop;
payment, plan/permission edge cases, time-sensitive behaviour or interrupted
requests. A transient disabled registration button was seen, but no systematic
loading, failure, validation, keyboard, responsive or contrast verification was
performed.
